# Credits

ReClonk is a free, non-profit, community reimagining of **Clonk**, the game that
shaped many of our childhoods. None of it would exist without the people below.

## Clonk

- **Matthes Bender**, creator of Clonk, and **RedWolf Design GmbH** (http://www.clonk.de)
- Clonk Rage engine coders: Matthes Bender, Sven Eberhardt, Peter Wortmann,
  Günther Brammer, Armin Burgmeier, Julian Raschke, Alexander Post
- Everyone who made Clonk's content, scenarios and community over the years

## LegacyClonk

- **The LegacyClonk Team and contributors** (https://github.com/legacyclonk/LegacyClonk),
  who keep the Clonk Rage engine alive and published its game content under CC BY-NC 4.0.

## Graphics

- The clonk, rock, gold and flint sprites, material textures and sky are the original
  Clonk Rage graphics by **RedWolf Design**, used under CC BY-NC 4.0 via LegacyClonk's
  content release. See [`public/classic/README.md`](public/classic/README.md).

- Started by Kevin (ripkiwi), with contributions from the community.
