# Credits

ReClonk is a free, non-profit, community reimagining of **Clonk**, the game that
shaped many of our childhoods. None of it would exist without the people below.

## Clonk

- **Matthes Bender**, creator of Clonk, and **RedWolf Design GmbH** (http://www.clonk.de)
- Clonk Rage engine coders: Matthes Bender, Sven Eberhardt, Peter Wortmann,
  Günther Brammer, Armin Burgmeier, Julian Raschke, Alexander Post
- Everyone who made Clonk's content, scenarios and community over the years

## LegacyClonk

- **The LegacyClonk Team and contributors** (https://github.com/legacyclonk/LegacyClonk),
  who keep the Clonk Rage engine alive and published its game content under CC BY-NC 4.0.

## Graphics, sounds, music and font

- The clonk, rock, gold, flint, flag, wooden hut and basement sprites, the Melee goal picture, material textures, sky, menu backgrounds
  and buttons, clonk portraits and rank symbols are the original Clonk Rage graphics by **RedWolf Design**,
  used under CC BY-NC 4.0 via LegacyClonk's content release.
- The main menu picture is by **Matthes Bender** and **Christopher Graulich**.
- The music (*Frontend*, the menu theme, and *Pizza Strings*, *Rocking-Horse* and *Sloping Off*,
  played during a round) is the original Clonk Rage soundtrack by **RedWolf Design**, under the same license.
- Sounds, the Endeavour font and the clonk names list are also original Clonk Rage content
  by **RedWolf Design**, under the same license.
- See [`public/classic/README.md`](public/classic/README.md) for every file and where it came from.
